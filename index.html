Plant care that fits your routine.

Planned features focus on practical watering, clear status and safer operation for potted plants.

📱

Remote watering

A simple interface to request watering or stop the pump, once connected to compatible hardware.

💧

Water-level awareness

A planned tank sensor can warn when the reservoir is low and help prevent the pump running dry.

📞

CallCare alerts

A future SIM-enabled add-on could place a call or send an alert when supported sensors detect a defined problem.

How PlantPilot could work

The first prototype will focus on one pot, a small reservoir and safe pump control.

01

Measure

Optional sensors check soil moisture and water level and report basic conditions.

02

Decide

The controller checks user commands and safety rules, such as a maximum watering duration.

03

Water & alert

A mini pump moves water from a reservoir. Alerts can be added after the hardware is tested.
